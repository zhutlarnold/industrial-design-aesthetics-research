# 生活中的工业设计：美学与功能 / Industrial Design Aesthetics and Function

中英文项目说明 / Bilingual project statement · 2026-10-06 (Asia/Shanghai)

## 项目目标 / Project purpose

用准确美术概念与真实产品图，帮助无专业背景读者理解工业设计美感；兼顾形式、功能和有证据的设计意图。

Help readers without formal art training understand industrial-product aesthetics through accurate concepts, real product imagery, function and evidence-based design intent.

## 功能与方法 / Capabilities and method

单件细读、同类比较、术语白话解释、偏好数据核查、设计意图、原图、长短图文、自然改稿、历史去重及分享交付。

Single-product readings, peer comparisons, accessible concepts, preference-data checks, design intent, source imagery, long/short articles, revision, history deduplication and shareable delivery.

这是复用研究、分析与交付流程的Skill，不是训练出的专用模型。公开相关项目的方法已有重合，项目差异在具体任务组合、证据规则与迭代要求，不宣称市场首创或零相似。

This is a reusable research, analysis and delivery workflow, not a specially trained model. Related public workflows overlap with parts of the method; the project’s distinction lies in its task combination, evidence rules and iterative requirements, not an unverified claim of market originality.

## 项目发起者贡献 / Project initiator’s contribution

提出生活产品美学方向；质疑单一Volvo例子，要求同类比较与数据口径；确定专业知识应通俗解释、文字充分展开且不强制哲思；审阅案例并决定先发布初版再迭代。

Defined the everyday-product aesthetics direction, challenged the single-Volvo example, requested peer comparison and bounded evidence, specified accessible professional explanations and fuller writing, and reviewed the trials before commissioning an initial skill.

上述角色依据本项目需求与反馈过程整理，未冒称独立编写全部代码。AI辅助完成网络资料整理、草稿、文件与脚本实现、打包和工具检查；项目发起者负责方向、评审标准和反馈决策。正式用于申请时应按自己的实际参与情况表达，不把AI参与抹去。

These roles are documented from the project’s requirements and feedback history; they do not imply sole authorship of all code. AI assistance supported source synthesis, drafts, file/script implementation, packaging and tool-based checks. The initiator supplied direction, review criteria and feedback decisions. An application statement should reflect actual participation and retain this attribution.

## 可检查产出 / Inspectable evidence

车灯五种设计比较及Juicy Salif单件分析，均提供长短图文；案例图文版式与图片检查记录保留，Skill验证范围见VALIDATION.md。

A five-design headlight comparison and a single-product Juicy Salif reading, each with full and shorter illustrated editions. Image/layout checks and skill validation limits are recorded in VALIDATION.md.
